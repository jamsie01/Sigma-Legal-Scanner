# Roadmap

Future expansion plans for the Sigma Legal Vacancy Scanner:

1. **Additional Law Firms**: Expand coverage to 50+ UK and US firms operating in London.
2. **Alerting System**: Integrate email and messaging notifications when new qualified vacancies match target criteria.
3. **Enhanced Deduplication**: Persistent cross-run diffing to track newly posted and closed vacancies over time.
4. **Automated Scheduling**: Cron-compatible runner for daily scheduled morning scans.
5. **Dashboard**: Lightweight web dashboard for filtering and viewing active roles.
